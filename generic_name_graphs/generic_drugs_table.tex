\section{Key Findings}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Total Dataset:} 331 patients across 9 generic drugs
\item \textbf{Most Prescribed:} Esomeprazole with 214 patients (64.7\% of total)
\item \textbf{Age Distribution:} Most patients fall in the 21-60 age range
\item \textbf{Gender Balance:} Overall slight female predominance (53.5\% female vs 46.5\% male)
\item \textbf{Peak Age Group:} 41-50 years shows highest patient count across multiple drugs
\end{itemize}

\section{Methodology}

This analysis was conducted on a dataset of 331 patients prescribed various generic drugs. Age groups were categorized into 10-year intervals, and gender distribution was analyzed for each generic drug. The data provides insights into prescription patterns and patient demographics for proton pump inhibitors and related medications.
