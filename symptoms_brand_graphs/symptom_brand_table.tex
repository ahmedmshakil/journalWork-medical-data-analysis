\section{Methodology}

This analysis examined the relationship between patient symptoms and prescribed brand medications using a dataset of 331 patients. The top 15 most common symptoms were analyzed for brand preferences, gender distribution, and treatment effectiveness. Brand effectiveness was measured as the percentage of patients with a specific symptom who received the most commonly prescribed brand for that symptom.
